About ProtocolScout

AI-Powered Clinical Trial Protocol Compliance Checker for Indian Regulations

What is ProtocolScout?

ProtocolScout is an intelligent compliance checking system that helps clinical trial researchers ensure their protocols meet Indian regulatory requirements.

Using advanced AI technologies from Amazon Web Services (AWS), ProtocolScout automatically analyzes clinical trial protocols and identifies potential compliance gaps against regulations from ICMR, CDSCO, NDCT, ICH-GCP, and the DPDP Act 2023.

Technology Stack

Amazon Bedrock (Claude 3.5)

Advanced AI reasoning engine for intelligent compliance analysis and rule extraction

Amazon Textract

OCR technology to extract text, tables, and forms from PDF protocols

Amazon Comprehend Medical

Medical entity extraction for drugs, conditions, procedures, and dosages

Amazon DynamoDB

Fast, scalable database for storing compliance rules and results

AWS Lambda

Serverless compute for processing protocols without managing servers

Amazon S3

Secure, encrypted storage for protocols and compliance reports

How It Works

Phase A: Regulatory Database
  1. Scrape regulatory sources (ICMR, CDSCO, etc.)
  2. Extract text from PDFs using Textract
  3. Parse regulations using Bedrock AI
  4. Store 50+ rules in DynamoDB
Phase B: Protocol Analysis
  1. User uploads protocol PDF
  2. Textract extracts text and tables
  3. Comprehend Medical extracts entities
  4. Bedrock checks compliance
  5. Generate detailed report

Regulatory Coverage

ICMR Guidelines 2017

National Ethical Guidelines for Biomedical and Health Research involving Human Participants

CDSCO Regulations

Central Drugs Standard Control Organization requirements for clinical trials

NDCT Rules 2019

New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019

ICH-GCP E6(R2)

International Council for Harmonisation Good Clinical Practice Guidelines

Schedule Y

Requirements and guidelines for clinical trials in India

DPDP Act 2023

Digital Personal Data Protection Act for data privacy compliance

Key Features

  • Automated Compliance Checking: Check against 50+ regulatory rules automatically
  • Medical Entity Extraction: Identify drugs, conditions, procedures, and dosages
  • Severity Classification: Violations categorized as Critical, High, Medium, or Low
  • Actionable Recommendations: Get specific guidance on fixing violations
  • Multiple Report Formats: Download reports in PDF, DOCX, or JSON
  • Secure & Encrypted: All data encrypted at rest and in transit
