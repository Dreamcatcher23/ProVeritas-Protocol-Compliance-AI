# Clinical Trial Protocol - Sample for Testing

## Protocol Information
- **Protocol Number**: TEST-2026-001
- **Protocol Title**: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study to Evaluate the Safety and Efficacy of Drug X in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes
- **Version**: 1.0
- **Date**: March 9, 2026
- **Sponsor**: Test Pharmaceutical Company
- **Principal Investigator**: Dr. Test Investigator, MBBS, MD

## 1. Study Objectives

### Primary Objective
To evaluate the efficacy of Drug X in reducing HbA1c levels in patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus over 12 weeks.

### Secondary Objectives
- To assess the safety and tolerability of Drug X
- To evaluate changes in fasting blood glucose levels
- To assess quality of life improvements

## 2. Study Design
- **Study Type**: Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled
- **Study Duration**: 12 weeks treatment + 4 weeks follow-up
- **Sample Size**: 200 participants (100 per arm)
- **Study Sites**: 5 sites across India

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### Inclusion Criteria
1. Male or female participants aged 18-65 years
2. Diagnosed with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus for at least 6 months
3. HbA1c levels between 7.5% and 10.5%
4. BMI between 25 and 40 kg/m²
5. Willing to provide written informed consent

### Exclusion Criteria
1. Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus
2. Pregnant or breastfeeding women
3. Severe renal impairment (eGFR < 30 mL/min)
4. History of diabetic ketoacidosis
5. Participation in another clinical trial within 30 days

## 4. Investigational Product

### Drug X (Active Treatment)
- **Dosage**: 100 mg once daily
- **Route**: Oral tablet
- **Duration**: 12 weeks
- **Manufacturer**: Test Pharma Ltd.

### Placebo
- Matching placebo tablets
- Same administration schedule

## 5. Informed Consent

All participants will provide written informed consent before any study procedures. The informed consent form includes:

1. Study purpose and procedures
2. Voluntary participation statement
3. Right to withdraw at any time without penalty
4. Potential risks and benefits
5. Confidentiality measures
6. Compensation for study-related injury
7. Contact information for questions
8. Data privacy and protection measures
9. Use of biological samples
10. Insurance coverage details

Participants will be given adequate time to review the consent form and ask questions. Consent will be obtained by qualified study personnel.

## 6. Study Procedures

### Screening Visit (Week -2)
- Informed consent
- Medical history
- Physical examination
- Vital signs
- Laboratory tests (HbA1c, FBG, lipid profile, renal function, liver function)
- ECG

### Baseline Visit (Week 0)
- Randomization
- Dispense study medication
- Baseline assessments

### Follow-up Visits (Weeks 4, 8, 12)
- Vital signs
- Adverse event assessment
- Concomitant medication review
- Laboratory tests
- Drug accountability

### End of Study Visit (Week 16)
- Final assessments
- Safety follow-up

## 7. Safety Monitoring

### Adverse Event Reporting
All adverse events will be recorded and assessed for severity and causality.

### Serious Adverse Events (SAE)
Serious adverse events must be reported to CDSCO within 24 hours of becoming aware of the event. SAEs include:
- Death
- Life-threatening events
- Hospitalization or prolongation of hospitalization
- Persistent or significant disability
- Congenital anomaly

### Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB)
An independent DSMB will review safety data at regular intervals.

## 8. Ethics and Regulatory Compliance

### Ethics Committee Approval
This protocol will be submitted to the Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) for approval before study initiation. The study will be conducted in accordance with:
- ICMR National Ethical Guidelines for Biomedical and Health Research, 2017
- ICH-GCP E6(R2) Guidelines
- New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019
- Schedule Y requirements

### Regulatory Approval
The study will be registered with CTRI (Clinical Trials Registry India) and CDSCO approval will be obtained before enrollment.

### Data Privacy
All participant data will be handled in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Personal data will be:
- Collected only with explicit consent
- Stored securely with encryption
- Retained only for the necessary period
- Accessible only to authorized personnel

## 9. Statistical Analysis

### Sample Size Calculation
200 participants (100 per arm) provides 90% power to detect a 0.5% difference in HbA1c reduction.

### Analysis Populations
- Intent-to-Treat (ITT)
- Per-Protocol (PP)
- Safety Population

### Statistical Methods
- Primary endpoint: ANCOVA with baseline HbA1c as covariate
- Secondary endpoints: t-tests, chi-square tests
- Safety data: Descriptive statistics
